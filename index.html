Our Story

Good Food.
Good People.

Ember & Oak started with one simple idea: a great burger can bring people together. We are a fictional neighborhood grill inspired by backyard cookouts, fresh ingredients, and meals shared with friends.

Our goal is to serve delicious food in a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere where everyone feels at home.

  • Fresh ingredients prepared with care
  • A family-friendly atmosphere
  • Bold flavors and generous portions
